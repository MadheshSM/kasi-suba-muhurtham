About Us

One Team, Built on the Oldest Traditions

Kasi Suba Muhurtham is an A to Z event management business on East Raja Street, Kancheepuram. We arrange traditional weddings and family functions from start to finish — catering, nadaswaram and melam, flower and stage decoration, photography, seer arrangements, transport and every ritual item in between.

We do not spread ourselves thin. We take on a limited number of functions at a time, which is exactly why your family gets a proper site visit, a written quote and someone who actually answers the phone on the morning of the muhurtham.

  • One point of contact for all eleven services
  • Itemised written quote before you commit
  • Local team that knows Kancheepuram halls and customs
  • Every task scheduled around your muhurtham time
