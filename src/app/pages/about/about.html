Our Story

One Team, With Standards Borrowed From the Old

Kasi Suba Muhurtham was started to solve a problem every family in Kancheepuram knows well: arranging a wedding means dealing with a dozen separate people. The cook, the melam party, the flower shop, the photographer, the van driver, the person who brings the pooja items — all of them coordinated by an uncle who should be enjoying the function instead.

We took the eleven arrangements that matter most and put them under one roof. You give us the date, the function and the guest count. We handle catering, nadaswaram and melam, flower, balloon and stage decoration, photo and video, bridal and groom preparation, ritual arrangements, DJ and music, seer and gifts, transport, fireworks and welcome counters.

What we can promise is the thing larger outfits struggle with — that the person who quoted your function is the same person who picks up the phone at 6 AM on the morning of the muhurtham.
